Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Intrinity Fire Protection LLC ("Intrinity," "we," "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information this website collects, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

We only collect information you choose to send us:

  • Service requests: your name, phone number, email address, company, property location, and the details you write about the job.
  • Job applications: your name, phone number, email address, the city and position you are applying for, your experience, licenses or certifications, driver's license status, any notes, and a resume if you attach one.

This website does not use analytics or advertising cookies.

How We Use It

  • To respond to your request, quote work, schedule service, and follow up.
  • To review job applications and contact applicants.
  • To keep basic business records.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising.

Service Providers

Forms on this site are delivered by Formspree, which processes submissions and any attached files on our behalf. The site is hosted by a third-party web host that may log standard technical data such as IP addresses for security. These providers handle data under their own privacy policies.

How Long We Keep It

We keep service requests as long as needed to serve you and maintain business records. We keep job applications for as long as needed to fill positions and meet record-keeping obligations, then delete them.

Your Rights, Including Under Texas Law

Under the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act and similar laws, you may have the right to:

  • Confirm whether we hold personal information about you and access it.
  • Correct inaccurate information.
  • Delete information you provided to us.
  • Get a copy of your information in a portable format.
  • Opt out of the sale of personal information or targeted advertising. We do neither.

To make a request, email orion@intrinityfire.com or call (254) 613-4231. We will respond within 45 days. If we decline a request, you may appeal by replying to our decision, and we will respond to the appeal within 60 days.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect the information you send us. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
