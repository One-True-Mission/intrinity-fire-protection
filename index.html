Thirty Years in the Trade, Now Under One Name

Intrinity Fire Protection is a growing Texas fire protection contractor backed by 30 years of industry experience. We inspect, repair, and install the systems that keep a building open and its people safe.

Most of our work is commercial: offices, retail, restaurants, multifamily, and new construction for the general contractors building them. We also take care of homeowners with residential sprinklers and backflow assemblies. Either way, one company handles the inspection, the report, and the repair, so you are not calling three vendors to close out one deficiency.
