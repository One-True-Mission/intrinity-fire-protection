A Sprinkler System Only Counts If It Works

Sprinklers sit untouched for years, which is exactly why they need regular inspection. A closed valve, a corroded head, or a dry pipe valve that will not trip can leave a building unprotected without anyone knowing.

What we check

What a Sprinkler Inspection Covers

The exact list depends on your system and how often it is due, but a typical inspection includes:

  • Control valves, confirmed open and properly secured
  • Gauges, checked for normal air and water pressure
  • Waterflow alarms and supervisory switches, tested
  • Sprinkler heads, checked for damage, corrosion, paint, and obstructions
  • Fire department connection, checked for access and condition
  • Main drain test, compared against past results
  • Dry pipe valve and air supply, on dry systems
